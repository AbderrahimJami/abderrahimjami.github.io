Hello 👋, I'm Abderrahim, an Unreal Engine programmer based in Edinburgh. I write gameplay and simulation systems in C++ and Blueprints, and I've spent the last few years building things in UE5, both at work and in my own time.

## What I do

I'm currently an Unreal Engine Developer at **Leonardo UK**, in the Synthetics team. I joined while the team was being formed and led its early Unreal Engine development. Day to day, I build proofs of concept, interactive demonstrations and UE5 training environments. I also act as the team's Unreal specialist, helping new joiners get up to speed and sharing knowledge across the team.



## Outside of work

When I'm not programming, you'll probably find me playing video games, entering game jams, playing table tennis or watching films. I speak English and Italian.
