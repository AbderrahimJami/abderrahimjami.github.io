Part-time role as Lead Gameplay Programmer at [Future Realm Games](https://www.linkedin.com/company/future-realm-gamers/), an indie studio founded in 2024 and "built by passionate players, for passionate players."

### Overview
**Neon Reverie** is a story-driven cyberpunk game about family, survival and two collapsing realities. After a catastrophic AI event, two siblings are separated across two worlds: Aurora, a teenage girl, and her older brother Kael, a rogue mechanic in a dying megacity. The player switches between them.

The game is being built by a small, globally distributed team, working towards a 30-minute playable demo.

### My role
- Lead Gameplay Programmer (part-time)
- Responsibilities: 
  - Leading a programming team of 4 people
  - Contributing to the codebase and implementing gameplay features
  - Liaising with other department leads during production
  -   Steering technology adoption and performing code reviews 



### Technical highlights
- Engine: Unreal Engine 5
- Languages: C++/Blueprints
- Systems: 
  - GAS
  - AI Perception
  - StateTree
  - Gameplay Cameras
  - CommonUI & UMG
