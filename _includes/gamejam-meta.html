{%- comment -%} Join the non-empty `jam` front matter fields of a post with " · ". Arguments: jam - required, the post's `jam` hash fields - required, comma-separated field names, e.g. 'name,duration' {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _parts = '' | split: '' -%} {%- assign _fields = include.fields | split: ',' -%} {%- for _field in _fields -%} {%- assign _value = include.jam[_field] | strip -%} {%- if _value != '' -%} {%- assign _parts = _parts | push: _value -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {{- _parts | join: ' · ' | escape -}}
